Conditions:
~~~~~~~~~~~~~
1) For TMs 1 to 6 DeModPorts ~ CQIports ~ CRSports.(treat ~ same as = (as = has creates parsing problems))
2) For TMs 7 to 9 DeModPorts ~ DMRSports, CQIports ~ CSIRSports.
3) #CQIports (CRS/CSIRS) can be either {1,2,4} or {1,2,4,8} #ports respectively.
4) In case of TM 5 (CL-MU-MIMO) specify nLayers=1;
5) For all TMs nCodeWords=1;
6) For TMs 7,8,9 #DMRSports must be equal to nLayers=2;
7) DemodulationScheme is preferably MMSE for all TMs except TM 2 where MRC is preferred.

Limitations:
~~~~~~~~~~~~~
1) TM 5 (CL-MU-MIMO) cannot support more than 2 UEs
2) TM 9 cannot support single CQIport setting (DeModPorts ~ "7", CQIports ~ "15") this can be simulated in TM 7.
3) CRS is not transmitted in case of TMs 7,8,9.

Explanation:
~~~~~~~~~~~~~
1) CRS: not precoded, DMRS: precoded, CSIRS: not precoded.
2) DeModPorts are ports used for DeModulation at the receiver. This can be either CRS/DMRS but not CSIRS (as it is not precoded and the precoder is not transparent in TMs 8,9 (expect possibly in TM 7)).
3) CQIports are used for CSI computation at the receiver. This can be either CRS/CSIRS but not DMRS (as we need raw channel and DMRS gives only precoded channel).
4) Difference between CRS based CL schemes(TMs 4,5,6) and CSIRS based CL schemes(TMs 8,9) is, with CRS though it is CL the precoder is transparent to the UE but with CSIRS it is not transparent and requires additional DMRS for demodulation.
